# Problem Statement

## Background

HSR Motors receives customer leads from multiple channels including:

- Facebook Ads
- Google Ads
- Twitter
- Company Website
- Offline Events

Currently, all leads are managed manually using spreadsheets.

## Existing Problems

- No real-time collaboration
- Difficult to track lead status
- No centralized customer information
- No analytics dashboard
- Difficult follow-up management
- Duplicate lead entries
- Manual reporting

## Goal

Design a modern Lead Management CRM that enables:

- Efficient lead handling
- Faster follow-ups
- Better collaboration
- Actionable dashboards
- Improved sales productivity
