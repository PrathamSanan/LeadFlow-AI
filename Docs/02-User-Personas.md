# User Personas

## Persona 1 - Sales Executive

### Goals

- Call customers quickly
- Update lead status
- Schedule follow-ups
- Track conversations

### Pain Points

- Searching spreadsheets
- Missing follow-ups
- Duplicate data
- Slow updates

---

## Persona 2 - Business Manager

### Goals

- View sales performance
- Analyze lead sources
- Monitor conversion rate
- Measure team productivity

### Pain Points

- No dashboards
- Manual reports
- Difficult decision making
