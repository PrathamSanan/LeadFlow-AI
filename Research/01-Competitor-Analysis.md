# Competitor Analysis

## HubSpot CRM

### Strengths
- Clean UI
- Easy lead management
- Excellent contact timeline
- Powerful dashboard

### Weaknesses
- Too many features for beginners
- Navigation can feel overwhelming

---

## Salesforce

### Strengths
- Highly customizable
- Powerful reporting
- Enterprise ready

### Weaknesses
- Complex interface
- High learning curve

---

## Zoho CRM

### Strengths
- Affordable
- Good lead pipeline
- Nice automation

### Weaknesses
- Outdated visual design
- Crowded screens

---

## Pipedrive

### Strengths
- Excellent sales pipeline
- Easy to use
- Great visual workflow

### Weaknesses
- Limited analytics
